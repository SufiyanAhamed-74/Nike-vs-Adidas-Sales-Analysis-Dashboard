# Nike vs Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard** designed to analyze and compare **Nike and Adidas products** based on pricing, ratings, discounts, reviews, and price segments.

The dashboard transforms product-level data into an easy-to-understand business intelligence report that helps identify pricing patterns, discount trends, brand differences, and high-priced products.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Compare Nike and Adidas product performance.
- Analyze average sale prices and discounts by brand.
- Understand product distribution across different price segments.
- Analyze product ratings and customer reviews.
- Identify the top 10 highest-priced products.
- Provide interactive filtering for business-focused analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPI cards, slicers, and comparisons

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Price and Rating Comparison
Compares product pricing and ratings to understand the relationship between product price and customer rating.

### 2. Average Discount by Brand
Shows the average discount offered by Nike and Adidas and makes brand-level discount comparison easier.

### 3. Average Sale Price by Brand
Compares the average sale price of Nike and Adidas products.

### 4. Product Distribution by Price Segment
Analyzes how products are distributed across:
- Budget
- Mid Range
- Premium

### 5. Top 10 Highest-Priced Products
Highlights the products with the highest listed prices for quick identification of premium-priced products.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard using:
- **Main Brand**
- **Price Segment**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard dynamically.
